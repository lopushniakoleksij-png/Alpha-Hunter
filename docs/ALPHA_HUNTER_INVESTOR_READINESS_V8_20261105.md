# ALPHA HUNTER — INVESTOR BRIEF | V8 RESEARCH TERMINAL

**Status:** Internal due-diligence draft, 5 November 2026. Not an investment offer, financial promotion, valuation, independently audited return claim, or authority to deploy.

## Executive summary

Alpha Hunter is a cryptocurrency market-observation and **paper-research** platform being developed as an evidence-first product. Its V8 web interface presents canonical market observations, research watchlists, historical paper results, and system-health diagnostics. The underlying V7.2 research engine, scheduled scanner and existing research data are separate from this isolated frontend.

**Commercial hypothesis:** Experienced research users and teams may value transparent evidence provenance, consistent observational workflows and careful separation between setup scores, paper models and actual executable trades. This has **not** been validated by paid customer research.

### What we do not claim

No verified customer revenue or user growth; no proven win-rate, predictive probability, independently audited P&L or live trade profitability; no verified transferability of every dependency; no current investor commitment. **Paper returns do not equal realised investment performance.**

## Product positioning and potential market

Primary research concept: **explainable, source-aware crypto market intelligence and shadow strategy research**, not trading execution software. Potential future customers could include self-directed professional researchers, small digital-asset research teams, and educational/paper-trading communities, subject to market testing and applicable terms and laws.

Proposed future product routes **for validation only**: research dashboard subscriptions, B2B research reporting/licensing, or an asset sale of appropriate IP and tooling. No price, addressable market size, conversion rate or revenue forecast is claimed without evidence.

## Investment-readiness gates

1. **Product gate:** Exact V8 branch-HEAD CI PASS for browser, keyboard, accessibility, mobile and recovery mode. Independently review real-device usability; obtain explicit owner authorization for controlled **web-only** pilot and rollback. Never interrupt scanner or modify live database.
2. **Evidence gate:** Independently validate signal source identity, historical record boundaries, repeatable paper fill accounting, realistic costs, correlation/selection bias and a held-out prospective cohort. No claims based on preliminary paper statistics alone.
3. **Security/operations gate:** Independent PostgreSQL 17 exact public/private isolated trigger benchmarking and separate security reviewer approval ([Issue #420](https://github.com/lopushniakoleksij-png/Alpha-Hunter/issues/420)); privilege and paper-side-effect checks; source custody; safe operator permissions.
4. **Commercial gate:** Obtain informed product feedback from actual target users and independently document willingness-to-pay before asserting demand.
5. **Finance/legal gate:** Establish actual rights to the code, datasets, brand and contracts, confirm relevant regulatory and data licensing position, and independently verify historical expenses, liabilities and ownership before fundraising.
6. **Financing gate:** Any amount sought, valuation, security/ownership interest, shareholder dilution or investor outreach requires a separate explicit owner decision and professional review where appropriate.
