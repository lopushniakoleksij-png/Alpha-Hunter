## Nominal stop-risk exposure (not verified execution)

Risk per candidate equals `1000 USDT × abs(SL - EP) / EP` under the unleveraged reference-notional assumption. It excludes fee/slippage excesses, gaps, correlated co-movement, liquidation risk and funding. If all three same-day opportunities had independently entered and all stopped, the **sum of reference stop distances** would have been:

| UTC date | BTC reference stop risk | ETH reference stop risk | SOL reference stop risk | All three sum |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| Oct 23 | 44.4999 USDT | 22.4878 USDT | 19.1651 USDT | **86.1528 USDT** |

These are **conditional stop-distance exposures**, not realized losses, aggregate portfolio margin requirements or actual maximum drawdowns. The unresolved Oct 21 BTC signal has nominal R:R ~0.000645; even a hypothetical TP touch would be overwhelmed by the baseline taker-fee/slippage assumptions. No live risk authorization follows from this analysis.

## Why this is not a full risk/profitability certification
